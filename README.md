## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a Full Stack Developer with **5+ years** of experience designing, testing and shipping web and mobile applications — several of them platforms used at **national scale** in Cameroon.

I've built production software for **government ministries**, **CAMRAIL**, the **Port of Douala** and the **Cameroon Civil Aviation Authority**, as well as for private-sector clients — and I care about how it's built: **2,000+ automated tests** across my recent projects, CI, and documentation.

- 🌐 **Portfolio (FR/EN, with case studies):** [jordan-tukum.pages.dev](https://jordan-tukum.pages.dev/en)
- 🏗️ **Currently:** Lead Full Stack Developer at **ADWA SARL**, Douala — leading teams of 3–6 developers (Agile)
- 🌍 **International exposure:** official delegate of the CCAA — **AFI Aviation Week 2025**, Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe
