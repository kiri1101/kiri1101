## 🏆 Featured Projects

### 🏛️ SERVICES PUBLICS — National e-Government Platform

> Centralized digital platform that consolidates and digitises all administrative procedures and non-tax revenue collection across Cameroonian government ministries. Built as part of a national digital transformation initiative.

**Stack:** Vue.js · Nuxt · Laravel · TypeScript
🔗 **[Live Platform →](https://services-publics.cm/services/publics)**

---

### ✈️ EQUIP4SAFETY — Aviation Equipment Exchange SaaS

> B2B SaaS marketplace for the sale and exchange of aviation equipment between ICAO member institutions worldwide. Commissioned by the **Cameroon Civil Aviation Authority (CCAA)**.

**Stack:** Vue.js · Laravel · TypeScript
🔗 **[View Demo →](https://demo.equip4safety.org/marketplace)**

---

### 🚂 CAMRAIL — Self-Service Train Ticketing System

> Web application and interactive kiosk software enabling self-service ticket purchases at CAMRAIL train stations in Douala and Yaoundé. Built for Cameroon's national railway operator.

**Stack:** Laravel · Vue.js · NativePHP

---

### 🏦 Port of Douala — Customs Tax Collection SaaS

> SaaS platform automating customs tax and toll fee collection at the **Port of Douala (PAD)** — one of Central Africa's busiest trade gateways.

**Stack:** Laravel · Vue.js · TypeScript

---

### 🏛️ Ministry of Public Service — Procedure Management System

> Document and administrative procedure management software for the **Ministry of Public Service and Administrative Reforms**, digitising internal workflows at a national scale.

**Stack:** Laravel · Vue.js · TypeScript

---

### 💱 International Fund Transfer — Banking SaaS

> SaaS digitising international fund transfer procedures for banking and financial institution clients, streamlining compliance and documentation workflows.

**Stack:** Laravel · Vue.js · TypeScript
